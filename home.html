About the Project

Writing a good CV is hard when you do not know how to format it. ProCV solves this: the user fills in a simple form, and the system automatically creates a well-structured, A4-sized CV with a modern design, so anyone can apply for jobs with confidence.

Main Features

1

Simple Step-by-Step Form

Personal details, profile photo, summary, phone, email and address in one clean form.

2

Optional Sections

Add work experience, education and projects only if you need them, and use Add buttons for more.

3

Smart Skill & Language Picker

Search from a big list, click to add, and rate your level from Beginner to Expert.

4

Links & Portfolio

Add GitHub, LinkedIn, Facebook or any other social link, plus your portfolio and project links.

5

Education Details

Record school, college or university name with degree, period and grade.

6

Instant PDF Download

Preview your CV on screen and download a professional PDF with one click.
